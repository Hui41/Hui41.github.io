# Sasakian Manifolds

Let $M$ be a pseudo-Hermitian manifold.

We write

$$
(M^{2m+1},H,J,\theta).
$$

## Example: Heisenberg Group

Consider the Heisenberg group $\mathbb{H}^m$.

Its standard contact form is

$$
\theta = dt +
\frac{i}{2}
\sum_{\alpha=1}^m
\left(
z^\alpha d\bar z^\alpha
-
\bar z^\alpha dz^\alpha
\right).
$$
